# Embeddings

## Concept
Dense vectors that capture the meaning of words or sentences.

## Formula / Core Idea
Similar meanings map to nearby vectors. Compare with cosine similarity.

## When to Use
Semantic search, recommendations, RAG.

## Example
king - man + woman is close to queen (Word2Vec).

## Key Points
- Word2Vec, GloVe, then contextual embeddings
- Sentence embeddings power semantic search
- Vector databases store and search embeddings
